Help

I lost my token

Ask a mentor. They can look it up. Don't sign up again under a new name, or your progress will be split across two rows.

"Logged in" never shows up / the first cell fails

Make sure you pasted the whole block from the sign-up page, including the !pip install line. If it still fails, go to Runtime → Restart session and run the cell again.

How do I see what my circuit does?

counts(qc) runs it 1000 times and shows the results. print(qc.draw()) draws it.

My submission said "Slow down"

Submissions are limited to one every few seconds per team. The notebook waits and retries for you, so just give it a moment.

Can I work with others?

Yes. Sign up once as a team and everyone uses the same token. Each team's best result counts.

Still stuck?

Raise your hand. Mentors are walking around.
